LFXX - AIRAC 2611
=================

NeoRadar is used for approach, tower and ground for now.

Package
- Centre displays (FIR, UIR, CTR ASRs) and centre login profiles are no longer shipped.
- Published to Cloudflare R2, served at https://pkg.neoradar.app/lfxx.
- Squawks from CCAMS (squawk.http in the manifest).
- The vIFF CDM plugin ships with the package; the CDM lists show its items.

Radar systems
- Two systems: cdg (Paris-CDG approach, LFPG and LFPB AVISOs) and neoirma (a stripless IRMA:
  every other approach and AVISO). Both share one tag layout and one ground look; they differ in
  colours and targets.
- Track colours follow CoFrance's IRMA and Paris-CDG rules. neoIRMA: departures pink, arrivals
  blue, other traffic grey. CDG: CoFrance's flows and letters (S/N by runway pair, B, O, A, V,
  R, G/P, two-letter departure codes by route point, T, Z, X).
- CDG letters above the disc for north arrivals (09/27) and north departures, below otherwise.
- Flight state on the callsign: white text on a darkened box of the track colour when assumed,
  on hand-off green when transferred to you; green text when transferred out.
- Flights CoFrance never filters, and flights assumed or in transfer, show through the altitude
  filter.
- Trails: the old CDG trail rings, in each track colour.
- Tags in the EuroScope font, with climb/descent/level arrows and the voice type (/r, /t).
- Every target correlated (correlation mode always).
- Route draw in CoFrance's route colours, labels on a dark plate.
- RDF circle settings.
- Lists readable: CoFrance's list colours, nearly opaque, 11 px text.

AVISOs
- Each AVISO draws its airport's vSMR AVISO (160 airports) and nothing else: surfaces, guidance
  lines, gates, taxiway names, terminals, TORA, in the palette the ASR asks for.
- LFPG East / West arrows draw only for the matching runway configuration (08/09 or 26/27).
- Gate labels appear from the opening zoom and hide only once zoomed well out.
- LFHY (Moulins) has no vSMR AVISO file: it draws the sector file's runways.
